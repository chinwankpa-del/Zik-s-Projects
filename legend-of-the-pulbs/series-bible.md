# Legend of the Pulbs — Series Bible

## Overview
A 3-book cartoony, fun, witty adventure comic series set on a massive alien planet.
Art style: Bouncy cartoony — thick outlines, round soft shapes, exaggerated expressions,
squash and stretch proportions, bright saturated colors.

---

## The World — Verdi's
- A planet similar to Earth but **100x bigger**
- Massive diverse biomes, vast oceans, towering forests, storm-filled skies
- Enormous scale means epic travel and adventure across all three books

---

## The Pulb Species — Design Notes
- **Height:** ~3 feet tall
- **No neck** — head sits directly on body
- **No arms** — interact with everything via short range telekinesis
- **No mouth** — communicate entirely through telepathy
- **One circular bulb** on top of head — same round shape for every Pulb
- Element shown through **bulb color and glow only**, never shape
- **Round, soft, squat body**
- **Short stubby legs** — bouncy when they walk
- Every Pulb is **born with a certain element**
- Bulb acts as an emotion indicator — brightens, dims, flickers based on feeling

### Species Variants — Tree Pulbs (Canopy Village)
- Body made entirely of **bark** — rough, woody, earthy tones instead of soft round flesh
- Same squat shape, stubby legs, no arms, no mouth
- Bulb is a **miniature tree** on top of the head — branches spread and move with emotion,
  leaves act as mood indicators (bright/full = happy, drooping = sad, stiff = alarmed, bare = traumatized)
- Communicate via telepathy same as normal Pulbs — green-tinted wavy bubbles
- Two other villages on Verdi's have their own unique bulb types (to be revealed later in the story)

### How Pulbs Communicate
- **Telepathy** — all spoken dialogue is telepathic, shown with wavy-edged speech
  bubbles that glow softly in the speaker's element color
- **Bulb** — shows raw emotion beneath what is being said
- **Body language** — posture, movement, and telekinetic field gestures carry expression
- Moments of silence have no bubble at all — just bulb and body

## The Cosmic Hierarchy

```
Cosmic Pulb          (celestial, half the size of Verdi's — ancient guardian of all existence)
     │
Ultimate Pulb        (slightly bigger than normal — guardian of order, has every element)
     │
World Eater Pulb     (celestial, half the size of Verdi's — ancient destroyer of worlds)
     │
Destruction Pulb     (slightly bigger than normal — World Eater's weapon and servant)
```

## Major Characters

### The Three Heroes
Best friends since high school.

| Name | Element | Bulb Color | Glow Effect | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wave** | Water | Blue / Teal | Soft rippling pulse | Calm, strategic, the anchor of the group |
| **Flame** | Fire | Orange-Red | Warm flickering glow | Bold, impulsive, heart of the team |
| **Volt** | Electricity + Wind (chosen one) | Yellow / White + faint shimmer | Sharp crackling sparks with wind ripple | Witty, fast-talking, unpredictable |

**Emotional Bulb States:**
- Wave — excited: glows brighter blue / scared: dims and flickers blue
- Flame — excited: flares hot orange / scared: shrinks and fades red
- Volt — excited: rapid-fire sparks / scared: stutters and turns pale yellow

**Walk Styles:**
- Wave — smooth and steady
- Flame — heavy and stompy
- Volt — fast and bouncy

---

**Volt — Special Note:**
Volt is the first Pulb in the history of Verdi's to be born with two elements.
His second element (wind) lay dormant until discovered by Sprout in Canopy Village
and unlocked through training with Elder Thanos.
Combined electricity and wind = storm-level lightning capable of destroying shadow.

---

### Canopy Village Characters

**Elder Thanos**
- Leader of Canopy Village and its council
- Tree Pulb — ancient gnarled tree bulb, thick heavy branches, wide and slow-moving
- Deep scar across his face — old and permanent, bark that never fully healed
- Carries quiet authority earned through experience, not rank
- Has encountered darkness before (source of scar — to be revealed)
- Speaks in dark green telepathic bubbles

**Sprout**
- Village scientist of Canopy Village
- Small and wiry, lighter bark than others
- Miniature tree covered in tiny leaves that twitch and move constantly — always processing
- Carries instruments fashioned from bark and vine
- The one who discovers Volt's dual-element nature
- Speaks in light green telepathic bubbles — excitable and precise

**The Shadow Tree**
- A great ancient tree corrupted by the Destruction Pulb's darkness trail
- Located three days walk from Canopy Village at the root of the oldest tree
- Commands minions made of shadow
- Its roots spread beneath the village, slowly weakening the Tree Pulbs
- Cannot be fought by Tree Pulbs — they cannot touch shadow
- Destroyed by Volt using combined electricity and wind

### The Servants

| | Ultimate Pulb | Destruction Pulb |
|---|---|---|
| **Size** | Slightly bigger than normal | Slightly bigger than normal |
| **Colors** | Rainbow — shifts constantly | Black with red stripes |
| **Bulb** | Cycles through all element colors | Deep black with red crackle |
| **Ability** | Every single element | Shadow control + creation |
| **Master** | Cosmic Pulb | World Eater Pulb |
| **Personality** | Wise, composed, powerful | Cold, calculating, ruthless |

**Why Destruction Pulb is terrifying:**
Before absorbing the Ultimate Pulb he already has two devastating abilities:
- Shadow control — manipulate darkness, disappear, attack from anywhere
- Creation — conjure anything from nothing

After absorbing the Ultimate Pulb's power in Book 1 he gains every element on top of that.
By Book 3 he is essentially unstoppable — which is why the heroes need the crystals
to restore reality rather than just beating him in a straight fight.

**The World Eater's Image:**
Since the World Eater is half the size of Verdi's, when his image bleeds into reality
in Book 3 his silhouette would be visible from anywhere on the planet —
like a second sun appearing in the sky, but wrong.

## 3-Book Story Structure

### Book 1 — "The Capture"
- Heroes are living normal post-high school life when signs of chaos start spreading
- They discover the Ultimate Pulb has been kidnapped by the Destruction Pulb
- They fight to rescue him but fail — Destruction Pulb begins draining his powers
- Ending: Destruction Pulb absorbs the Ultimate Pulb's powers, heroes realize the
  real threat is bigger than they imagined
- **Theme:** Realizing the world is bigger than your comfort zone

### Book 2 — "The Alliance"
- Destruction Pulb grows stronger as the drain on the Ultimate Pulb continues
- The world starts visibly warping toward the World Eater's image
- Heroes travel across the massive planet recruiting crucial allies
- Each ally brings a unique element or skill needed for the final battle
- Ending: Alliance assembled but the world is running out of time
- **Theme:** You can't do everything alone

### Book 3 — "World's End"
- The World Eater's image bleeds into reality — landscapes crack, skies split, biomes corrupt
- Ultimate Pulb is barely alive, almost fully drained
- Heroes and allies face the fully powered Destruction Pulb in all-out war
- Ultimate Pulb is freed and reclaims his power
- Heroes are gifted three elemental reality crystals to restore the world
- World Eater retreats back into the cosmic background
- **Ending:** World restored, heroes changed forever, World Eater still out there
- **Theme:** True power comes from friendship and sacrifice
